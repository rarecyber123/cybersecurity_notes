  🔓 Vulnerabilities & Exploits

$ define vulnerability
> "A weakness in a system or software"

$ define exploit
> "Code/program written to take advantage of a known vulnerability"

$ define patch
> "An update that fixes a vulnerability/bug"

$ nmap --scan target_network
> Scans and lists all OPEN PORTS on a device
> ⚠️ Used by attackers BEFORE launching an attack

💥 Common Cyber Attacks

+ DoS  (Denial of Service)
    One system floods target with traffic → shuts it down

+ DDoS (Distributed Denial of Service)
    Many systems (BOTNET) attack target together

+ SEO Poisoning
    Boosts a malicious site's search ranking to trick users into clicking

+ Blended Attack
    Combines multiple techniques at once
    e.g. Worm + Trojan + Spyware
