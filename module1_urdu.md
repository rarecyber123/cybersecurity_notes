# Introduction to Cybersecurity

## Cybersecurity 
  Networked systems aur data ko unauthorized access/harm se protect karne ki ongoing koshish.
 
 3 levels pe zaroori hai:

$ Personal level →
>  apni identity, data, devices safe rakhna

$ Corporate level →
> company ki reputation, data, customers protect karna
 
$ State/National level →
> national security, citizens ki safety

## Types of Hackers

## 🧢 Hacker Kise Kehte Hain?
Hacker wo banda hai jo computer system ke kamzor points dhoondta hai. Har hacker bura nahi hota. Kaam ke hisaab se 3 types hain.

---

### 1. White Hat Hackers ✅ - "Good Guys"

 **Kaam**:  
 
> Company khud hire karti hai apna system check karwane ke liye  
 **Goal**:
> 
> Security strong karna  
 **Legal**:
> 
> 100% Legal. Permission hoti hai  
**Job Title**:
> 
>  Ethical Hacker, Penetration Tester, Security Analyst  

 **Real Example**: 
 > Bank apne app ki security check karwane ke liye White Hat hire karta hai. Wo batata hai "Yahan se hack ho sakta hai, fix kar lo".

---

### 2. Black Hat Hackers ❌ - "Bad Guys"

**Kaam**: Bina permission system torhna, data chori karna  
**Goal**: Paise banana, nuksaan pohnchana  
**Legal**: 100% Illegal. Jail + Fine dono  
**Example**: Credit card data chori kar ke dark web pe bechna  

**FIA Cyber Crime** Pakistan mein in ko pakarta hai.

---

### 3. Gray Hat Hackers ⚠️ - "Beech Wale"

**Kaam**: Bina permission system check karte hain, lekin nuksaan nahi pohnchate  
**Goal**: Company ko baad mein batana aur naam kamana  
**Legal**: Illegal hai, kyun ke permission nahi li  
**Example**: Kisi website mein bug dhoond ke owner ko email kar dena "Apki site hack ho sakti hai"

**Risk**: Company case bhi kar sakti hai.

---

### 4. Bonus Types 

| Type | Kaam |
| --- | --- |
| **Script Kiddie** | Dusron ke banaye tools use karte hain. Khud coding nahi aati |
| **Hacktivist** | Kisi cause ke liye hack karte. Jaise website deface kar dena |
| **State-Sponsored** | Country ki government ke liye kaam karte. Dusre mulk pe attack |
| **Blue Hat** | Company bahir se bulati hai software launch se pehle bugs dhoondne |
