## McCumber Cube is a 3D model for information security that combines three dimensions:

 * **Security goals** (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability)  

* **Information states** (Storage, Processing, Transmission)

* **Safeguards** (Policy, Technology, Human factors)

  ---
  

## 📊 The Three Dimensions of the McCumber Cube

| **Dimension** | **Elements** | **Explanation** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Security Goals (CIA Triad)** | - Confidentiality<br>- Integrity<br>- Availability | - **Confidentiality**: Prevent unauthorized disclosure of information.<br>- **Integrity**: Ensure data accuracy and reliability.<br>- **Availability**: Guarantee timely access for authorized users. |
| **Information States** | - Storage (Data at rest)<br>- Processing (Data in use)<br>- Transmission (Data in transit) | - **Storage**: Data saved on disks, memory, or cloud.<br>- **Processing**: Data being manipulated or computed.<br>- **Transmission**: Data moving across networks. |
| **Safeguards** | - Policy & Procedures<br>- Technology<br>- Human Factors (Education/Training) | - **Policy & Procedures**: Administrative rules, incident response plans.<br>- **Technology**: Firewalls, encryption, intrusion detection.<br>- **Human Factors**: User awareness, training, vigilance. |


---

##💡 Insight:  

 - Even the best technology fails if users aren’t trained or policies aren’t enforced.
 - The human factor is often the weakest link 
 - BUt also the most powerful defense when properly educated.
